# Mission: Deliberate Prose and Grant Writing

## Why
Develop the judgment to draft, diagnose, and revise clear, persuasive professional prose and grant applications without outsourcing the thinking to AI. Use that judgment to make IMPACT's case accurately, plainly, and convincingly to funders and partners.

## Success looks like
- Diagnose why a sentence, paragraph, or document succeeds or fails, then revise it deliberately.
- Write professional prose that is direct, specific, natural, and appropriate to its reader and purpose.
- Independently plan, draft, and revise coherent grant applications, using AI as an editor or collaborator rather than a substitute for judgment.

## Constraints
- Experienced everyday and professional writer; no systematic prior instruction in grammar or prose craft.
- Initial phase is comprehensive knowledge acquisition with no required exercises, quizzes, or progression gates.
- Visual explanations, sentence maps, and annotated comparisons are preferred.

## Out of scope
- Creative-fiction workshop techniques and imitation of another writer's voice.
- Treating generic AI or institutional nonprofit language as a model of good prose.
