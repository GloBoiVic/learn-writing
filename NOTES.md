# Teaching Notes

- Prefers a complete-system view before practice; visual learner.
- Current preferences: direct, natural professional prose; specificity; efficient movement; visible thought structure; sentence variety.
- Dislikes formulaic AI, generic corporate/nonprofit language, empty inspiration, jargon, inflated polish, repetitive triplets, repeated “not X, but Y,” and mechanical transitions.
- Course phase: knowledge acquisition only. Do not quiz, gate, or require retrieval until the learner explicitly transitions to practice.
- Source handling: emails are the primary current-voice evidence; grant drafts reveal work genre and institutional language, not natural voice; admired prose informs taste, not imitation.
