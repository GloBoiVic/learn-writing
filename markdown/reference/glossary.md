# Glossary

Terms are useful only when they help you see and revise a real choice.

| Term | Working definition |
| --- | --- |
| clause | A group of words with a subject and finite verb; an independent clause can stand alone. |
| predicate | Everything said about the subject, including its verb and complements. |
| complement | A word or phrase that completes a verb's meaning, such as an object or subject complement. |
| modifier | A word, phrase, or clause that limits, describes, or otherwise specifies another element. |
| coordination | Joining units as grammatical peers, often with *and*, *but*, *or*, or a semicolon. |
| subordination | Making one clause depend on another to express time, cause, condition, contrast, or concession. |
| syntax | The arrangement of words, phrases, and clauses in a sentence. |
| cadence | The felt movement of prose created by syntax, stress, sound, and sentence shape. |
| nominalization | A noun formed from a verb or adjective, such as *implementation* from *implement*. |
| parallelism | Using matching grammatical forms for units readers should compare. |
| rhetorical situation | The writer, reader, purpose, medium, constraints, and stakes surrounding a piece of writing. |
| rhetoric | The deliberate use of language and structure for a reader, purpose, and situation. |
| cohesion | The links that make sentences and paragraphs feel connected. |
| voice | The recurring pattern of choices in a writer's syntax, vocabulary, pace, stance, specificity, and qualification. |
| tone | The writer's stance toward a particular reader and situation. |
| register | The expected level of formality and specialized vocabulary in a context. |
| antecedent | The noun or noun phrase a pronoun refers to. |
| aspect | The verb form that presents an action as ongoing, completed, or prior. |
| claim | A proposition the writer asks the reader to accept or act on. |
| evidence | Information that supports a claim when its relevance and limits are explained. |
| output | A direct product of activities: sessions held, records verified, or people served. |
| outcome | A change expected to result from the work: knowledge, behavior, condition, or system performance. |
| indicator | An observable sign used as evidence of an output or outcome. |
| measure | A defined method, source, timing, and unit for observing an indicator. |
