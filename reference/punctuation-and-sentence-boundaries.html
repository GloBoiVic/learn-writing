Punctuation and Sentence Boundaries

Find the clause structure and intended relationship before choosing the mark.

MarkRequirementChoice and effect
PeriodEnds an independent clause.Separates ideas and gives a full stop.
Comma + coordinatorJoins two independent clauses with and, but, for, or, nor, so, yet.Names a familiar, relatively light relationship.
SemicolonJoins closely related independent clauses.Maintains continuity without naming the relationship.
ColonFollows a complete clause before an explanation, list, or consequence.Creates expectation and emphasis.
DashOptional where syntax permits.Creates an interruption, turn, or emphatic addition.
Comma pairSets off nonessential material.Signals that the material comments on an already identified noun or clause.
Boundary test. Does each side have a subject and finite verb, and could each side stand alone? If yes, choose a period, semicolon, or comma plus coordinating conjunction based on the relationship.
