Revision Ladder

Move from the largest consequence for the reader to the smallest surface detail.

  1. Purpose: What must this reader understand, decide, or do?
  2. Structure: Does the order answer the reader's questions?
  3. Evidence: Are claims supported, interpreted, and appropriately qualified?
  4. Paragraph: Does each paragraph have one job and a clear handoff?
  5. Sentence: Are actors, actions, relationships, and emphasis visible?
  6. Voice: Does the wording sound specific, natural, and fit for this reader?
  7. Copyedit: Are usage, punctuation, terms, numbers, and citations consistent?
  8. Proof: Are final formatting and surface errors resolved?
