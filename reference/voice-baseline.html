Voice Baseline: Version 1

A provisional profile based primarily on the available personal emails. It describes observable habits, not a grade or permanent identity. Practice will test and revise it.

Evidence boundaries

EvidenceWhat it can showWhat it cannot show
Personal emailsCurrent natural professional habits: direct requests, concrete problem lists, practical context, modest tone.Full range across high-stakes writing or formal prose.
Submitted grantsWork genre, IMPACT context, funder questions, and institutional language.Natural individual voice when the drafts had significant AI assistance.
Admired writingTaste for forceful direction, concrete particulars, purposeful long sentences, clear turns, and an explicit point of view.A template to imitate; its polemical register will not fit every professional situation.

Working strengths to protect

  • Gets to the operational problem quickly and gives relevant context.
  • Uses concrete particulars: maintenance items, software names, observed discrepancies, and consequences.
  • Maintains a calm, respectful, low-theater tone even when making a complaint.
  • Shows an intuitive reader model: recipients receive enough information to act.

Craft opportunities

  • Sentence boundaries and agreement can make directness more reliable under speed.
  • Syntax and paragraph sequencing can turn a list of facts into a shaped case.
  • Sentence endings, transitions, and qualification can sharpen emphasis without making the prose ornate.
  • Professional requests can put the desired action even earlier while keeping the natural, practical tone.

How the voice can travel

ContextAdapt the registerKeep the underlying preference
Personal writingAllow more idiosyncrasy, reflection, imagery, or emotional range.Concrete observation and an honest point of view.
Professional writingPut the request, problem, or implication early; give enough context to act.Directness, practical detail, and calmness.
Grant writingMake the case accountable, evidence-based, and proportionate to the prompt.Specificity, visible action, respect for people, and honest limits.
Voice test. If a sentence sounds polished but could have been written by any organization, inspect its actors, particulars, and degree of certainty. Replace generic uplift with something only this writer or organization can responsibly say.
