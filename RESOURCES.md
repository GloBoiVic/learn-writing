# Prose and Grant Writing Resources

## Knowledge

- [Purdue OWL: Grammar](https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/general_writing/grammar/index.html)
  University writing-lab guidance on agreement, pronouns, verbs, modifiers, and usage. Use for: core mechanics and clear examples.
- [Purdue OWL: Commas](https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/general_writing/punctuation/commas/index.html)
  A concise, reputable account of comma structures and exceptions. Use for: sentence-boundary and modifier punctuation.
- [The Chicago Manual of Style Online](https://www.chicagomanualofstyle.org/home.html)
  Major professional style authority. Use for: publication-level punctuation, capitalization, quotation, and editorial judgment; consult the current edition for a house-style decision.
- [Joseph M. Williams and Joseph Bizup, _Style: Lessons in Clarity and Grace_](https://press.uchicago.edu/ucp/books/book/chicago/S/bo207248847.html)
  Foundational source for clarity, cohesion, emphasis, nominalizations, and sentence-level revision. Use for: prose craft and editing judgment.
- [Francis-Noel Thomas and Mark Turner, _Clear and Simple as the Truth_](https://press.princeton.edu/books/paperback/9780691147439/clear-and-simple-as-the-truth)
  A rigorous account of prose as clear presentation rather than performance. Use for: style, voice, and the reader-writer relationship.
- [UNC Writing Center: Paragraphs](https://writingcenter.unc.edu/tips-and-tools/paragraphs/)
  University guidance on paragraph unity, coherence, development, and controlling ideas. Use for: paragraph and document structure.
- [Digital.gov: Plain Language Guide Series](https://digital.gov/guides/plain-language/)
  U.S. government guidance on writing for a specified audience and making information understandable. Use for: professional nonfiction and accessibility.
- [William Zinsser, _On Writing Well_](https://www.harpercollins.com/products/on-writing-well-william-zinsser)
  Enduring nonfiction guide emphasizing clarity, humanity, and clutter removal. Use for: line editing and professional prose.
- [NSF Proposal & Award Policies and Procedures Guide: Proposal Preparation](https://www.nsf.gov/policies/pappg/24-1/ch-2-proposal-preparation)
  Primary funder guidance demonstrating that compliance, completeness, readability, and proposal-specific instructions are material requirements. Use for: proposal discipline and budgets.
- [Grants.gov: Learn Grants](https://www.grants.gov/learn-grants)
  U.S. government portal for funding-process orientation. Use for: understanding requirements, notices, and submission context; always prioritize the individual funder’s current instructions.
- [W.K. Kellogg Foundation, _Logic Model Development Guide_](https://www.wkkf.org/resource-directory/resources/2004/01/logic-model-development-guide)
  Practical, widely used source for linking activities, outputs, outcomes, and evaluation. Use for: program design and grant outcomes.
- [Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, _Practical Strategies for Culturally Competent Evaluation_](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/19002)
  Public-health authority on credible, context-aware evaluation. Use for: equity, stakeholders, measures, and evaluation planning.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [Grant Professionals Association](https://grantprofessionals.org/)
  Professional association with chapters, standards, and peer learning. Use for: grant-writing practice, funder norms, and feedback beyond this course.
- [Association of Writers & Writing Programs](https://www.awpwriter.org/)
  Longstanding writing community and conference network. Use for: craft conversations and finding a local or online writing group.
- Local: a nonprofit-grant peer review circle or writing group in Southeastern Wisconsin
  Use for: sharing a real draft, comparing funder interpretations, and building judgment from practitioners. Join only if and when useful.

## Gaps

- Each live funding opportunity is its own primary source. Before drafting a real application, add that funder’s guidelines, rubric, FAQs, and funded examples to this file.
